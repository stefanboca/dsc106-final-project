Project writeup

So far, we have outlined a main interactive visualization that tells an overarching narrative. From that we have built multiple sub-interactive visualizations that add to that narrative. We drew inspiration from the Visualizing MBTA Data page because we saw that it had a main visualization then it had sub visualizations that gave additional context. Also, doing this allows us to not have one extremely cluttered visualization.

I think the most challenging part of our project to design will be to make it "user friendly," aka making it intuitive to use while also easily telling a story as they go through the visualizations one by one. For example, our data looks both at yearly data, but then also within that yearly data there is day-by-day data. This leaves us with a lot of options for how we want to convey year by year, but also day by day, and even days over years and we have to be careful in choosing a visualization that can achieve this while also not being confusing. This is because making something easy to understand or intuitive isn't something that will be extremely obvious on first try and might take multiple tries.
